## What I'm building

### 7odor — time under control, teams connected

I lead the engineering behind **[7odor](https://7odor.com/en/)**, a Made-in-Morocco workforce management platform for organizations ranging from small teams to multi-site enterprises.

- Facial and location-aware attendance with configurable organization policies
- Scheduling, shifts, leave, approvals, employee records, and operational workflows
- Real-time dashboards, reporting, role-based access, and multi-site management
- Web, iOS, and Android experiences in Arabic, French, English, and Spanish
- Integrations with attendance terminals, access control, workspaces, and field operations
